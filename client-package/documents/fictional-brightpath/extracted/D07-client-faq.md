# Client FAQ

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D07. Version 1.1. Owner: Head of Client Services. Last reviewed: 2026-04-02.

## 1. Working with us

**1.1 How do we start?** After signing, your Account Lead runs an intake meeting within 2 working days. Onboarding follows the steps in D01.

**1.2 Who is my contact?** Your Account Lead is your main contact. Escalations are listed in D05.

**1.3 How do I request changes to a campaign?** Send the change in writing to your Account Lead. Changes to approved creative need a new approval (see D03).

## 2. Campaigns

**2.1 How long does a campaign take to launch?** Once all approvals are complete, a standard campaign launches within 3 working days.

**2.2 What do you need from us for a campaign?** A completed paid social brief (see D04) and written creative approval.

## 3. Reporting

**3.1 How often do we get reports?** Weekly reports show spend, reach, and KPI results.

## 4. Commercial

**4.1 What does the Growth retainer include?** 45 included hours each month, for a fee of $4,800.

**4.2 How do we cancel?** Give 30 calendar days' written notice to your Account Lead (see D06).
