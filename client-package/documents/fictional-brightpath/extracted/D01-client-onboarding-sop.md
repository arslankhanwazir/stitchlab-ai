# Client Onboarding SOP

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D01. Version 2.1. Owner: Head of Client Services. Last reviewed: 2026-03-01.

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how a signed client is set up in Brightpath systems, from contract to first campaign kickoff.

## 2. Intake meeting

2.1 The Account Lead holds the intake meeting within 2 working days of contract signature.

2.2 The intake meeting covers the client's goals, brand guidelines, approved channels, and the names of the client's own contacts.

2.3 The Account Lead records the meeting notes in the client's ClickUp space under "Intake."

## 3. Setting up the client in ClickUp

3.1 Create a new Space named with the client's legal name.

3.2 Add the standard folders: Briefs, Creative, Reporting, and Contracts.

3.3 Create the onboarding task list. Each task must be completed in order:

1. Create the Space and folders.
2. Upload the signed contract to Contracts.
3. Add the client contacts with their role and approval rights.
4. Complete the paid social brief (see D04).
5. Set up reporting access for the client.
6. Schedule the kickoff call.

3.4 The Systems Lead grants ad account access only after step 3 is complete.

## 4. Kickoff

4.1 The kickoff call runs for 60 minutes and includes the Account Lead, the Marketing Director, and the client's main contact.

4.2 A kickoff summary is sent to the client within 1 working day of the call.

## 5. Records

5.1 All onboarding records are kept in the client's ClickUp Space.

5.2 Records are retained for the length of the client relationship plus 24 months.
