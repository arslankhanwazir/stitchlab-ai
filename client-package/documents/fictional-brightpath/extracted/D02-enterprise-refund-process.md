# Enterprise Refund Process

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D02. Version 1.4. Owner: Finance Lead. Last reviewed: 2026-02-15.

## 1. Scope

This process applies to enterprise clients (retainer tier Enterprise, see D06) who request a refund of prepaid fees.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 A refund request is eligible only if the work has not started, or the client has formally cancelled under the retainer terms.

2.2 Refunds for completed work are not available.

## 3. How to request a refund

3.1 The client sends a written request to the account lead named in the contract. Verbal requests are not accepted.

3.2 The written request must state the invoice number, the amount requested, and the reason.

3.3 The account lead confirms eligibility and passes the client's written request to Finance.

## 4. Approval

4.1 The Finance Lead approves or declines the refund.

4.2 Approval decisions are made within 5 working days of receiving a complete request.

4.3 Finance confirms the decision to the account lead and the client in writing.

## 5. Payment

5.1 Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days of approval.

5.2 Payment timing depends on the bank processing time.
