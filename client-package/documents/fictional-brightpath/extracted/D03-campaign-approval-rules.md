# Campaign Approval Rules

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D03. Version 3.0. Owner: Marketing Director. Last reviewed: 2026-04-10.

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to every paid advertising campaign run on a client's ad account.

## 2. Who approves

2.1 The Account Lead prepares every campaign brief.

2.2 The Marketing Director approves campaign budgets above $15,000 per month.

2.3 Campaigns at or below $15,000 per month are approved by the Account Lead.

## 3. Budget and audience checks

3.1 Budget thresholds are set out in section 2 of this document.

3.2 Audience targeting must be checked against the client's brand guidelines before submission.

3.3 Audiences built from personal data need written confirmation from the client's data owner.

## 4. Creative sign-off

4.1 The Account Lead signs off all creative before it is sent to the client.

4.2 The client must approve creative in writing before the campaign goes live.

4.3 Creative changes after approval need a new approval.

## 5. Launch timing

5.1 Once all approvals are complete, a standard campaign launches within 3 working days.

5.2 Campaigns requested for urgent launch need written approval from the Head of Client Services.
