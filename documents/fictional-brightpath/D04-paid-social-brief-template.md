# Paid Social Brief Template

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D04. Version 1.2. Owner: Account Leads. Last reviewed: 2026-01-20.

## 1. Brief fields

Every paid social brief must include these fields:

1. Campaign objective (for example: awareness, leads, sales)
2. Target audience
3. Platforms (for example: LinkedIn, Meta, TikTok)
4. Key performance indicators (KPIs)
5. Total budget and daily spend
6. Start date and end date
7. Deliverables (creative formats and quantities)
8. Approval contacts

## 2. Approval rules

2.1 The Account Lead completes the brief and submits it in ClickUp.

2.2 A campaign with a total budget above $10,000 requires Marketing Director sign-off before launch.

2.3 Creative must be approved by the client before launch (see D03, section 4).

## 3. Reporting

3.1 Each campaign receives a weekly report showing spend, reach, and KPI results.

3.2 Final reports are issued within 5 working days of campaign end.
