# Retainer Pricing and Cancellation Terms

**Brightpath Media (demo). FICTIONAL SAMPLE.** Document ID: D06. Version 2.0. Owner: Managing Director. Last reviewed: 2026-02-01.

## 1. Retainer tiers

| Tier | Monthly fee | Included hours |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $2,500 | 20 |
| Growth | $4,800 | 45 |
| Enterprise | $9,500 | 100 |

## 2. Fees

2.1 Retainer fees are invoiced monthly in advance.

2.2 Additional hours beyond the included amount are billed at $95 per hour.

2.3 Ad spend is billed separately and is not part of the retainer fee.

## 3. Payment terms

3.1 Invoices are payable within 14 days of issue.

3.2 Late payments may pause work until the account is current.

## 4. Cancellation

4.1 A client may cancel a retainer by giving 30 calendar days' written notice to the Account Lead.

4.2 Work already delivered before the end of the notice period is invoiced as normal.

4.3 Refund requests are handled under the enterprise refund process (see D02).
